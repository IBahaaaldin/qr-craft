Key Phytochemical Mechanisms at a Glance
🪻 Lavender (Lavandula) Inhaled linalool/linalyl acetate acts via olfactory circuits to stimulate central amygdala GABAergic inhibitory neurons.
🍋 Lemon Balm (Melissa) Rosmarinic acid fraction inhibits GABA transaminase (GABA-T), increasing calming GABA levels in synaptic junctions.
🌿 Ashwagandha (Withania) Triethylene glycol (TEG) and withanolides accelerate sleep onset and increase deep non-REM restorative sleep.
🌸 Saffron (Crocus sativus) Crocin and safranal promote melatonin synthesis and sleep architecture quality (requires attention to drug-drug interactions).
